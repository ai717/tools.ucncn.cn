📖 Browser-Based Image Compression: WebP, JPEG & Privacy

Whether applying for government certifications, job portals, or submitting tax invoices, file upload limits (such as "must be under 2MB" or "maximum 500KB") are a frequent hurdle. Most online compressors upload your private photos to remote cloud servers. Our tool executes all compression algorithms directly inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and WebAssembly.

❓ How does client-side compression protect my personal privacy?

When you drop an image into our tool, the browser reads file bytes into local RAM via the FileReader and createImageBitmap APIs. Pixel manipulation, resizing, and encoding occur on your local CPU. No network requests are made, ensuring sensitive documents (IDs, passports, financial statements) remain completely private.

❓ Why should I convert images to WebP?

Developed by Google, WebP provides superior lossless and lossy compression for web images. WebP lossy images are 25–34% smaller than comparable JPEG images at equivalent visual quality, while supporting alpha transparency like PNG.

❓ Does compression remove EXIF camera metadata?

Yes! Canvas re-encoding automatically strips hidden EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates, camera model, lens parameters, and shoot dates), further protecting your personal privacy when sharing photos online.

❓ What is the recommended target size for email and job portals?


• Under 2MB: Standard ceiling for online job recruitment and university exam portals.
• Under 500KB: Ideal for email attachments, Slack, and messaging apps without noticeable quality loss.
• Under 200KB: Standard specification for visa applications, driver licenses, and passport avatars.
