Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, security, and tool operations.

❓ Are NoHeartburn Tools truly free forever? Will you add paywalls later?

Every tool on this website is completely free for personal, commercial, and educational use. We believe everyday utility tools should be universal public goods rather than subscription traps.

❓ Are my uploaded images or sensitive documents sent to remote servers?

No. Tools like Image Compressor and PDF Merger utilize modern browser APIs (HTML5 File API, WebAssembly, Canvas, and pdf-lib). All computation happens locally on your CPU/GPU. You can even disconnect your internet and verify they continue to function.

❓ Why don't I need an account to use these utilities?

Forcing users to register an account just to compress an image or convert a unit introduces unnecessary friction. We respect your time and provide instant utility with zero barriers.

❓ Which web browsers are supported?

All modern standards-compliant browsers are supported, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari, and Chromium-based mobile browsers on iOS and Android.
