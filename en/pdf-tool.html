📖 Client-Side PDF Operations: Privacy, Security & Standards

PDF (Portable Document Format) is the world's most widely adopted standard for official contracts, tax receipts, bank statements, and legal NDAs. Most free online PDF converters transmit your documents to third-party cloud servers where sensitive data may be cached or logged. Our PDF Merger and Splitter executes 100% in-browser using the WebAssembly-accelerated pdf-lib library.

❓ Why is local PDF manipulation safer for corporate NDAs and contracts?

When merging contracts or tax reports locally, file bytes are decoded in browser sandbox memory. Because no network transmission occurs, there is zero risk of data interception, compliance leaks (such as GDPR or HIPAA violations), or server storage retention. Once you close the tab, all memory is instantly reclaimed.

❓ Are there limits on the number of pages or file size?

Unlike commercial PDF platforms that lock multi-page exports behind monthly subscription paywalls, our client-side tools impose no artificial page limits. Processing capacity depends strictly on your device available RAM and CPU performance.

❓ How do custom page range inputs work (e.g. 1-3, 5, 8-10)?

You can type page ranges separated by commas or hyphens:
• 1-5: Extracts pages 1 through 5 sequentially.
• 1, 3, 5: Extracts specific individual pages.
• 1-3, 7, 10-12: Combines contiguous segments and single pages into a new unified PDF.

❓ Does this tool support password-encrypted PDFs?

Encrypted PDFs require cryptographic authentication before page structures can be modified. To merge or split password-protected documents, decrypt them with your PDF reader first before importing.
