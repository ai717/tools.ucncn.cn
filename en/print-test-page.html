📖 Printer Diagnostics Guide: CMYK Colors, Nozzle Clogs & Calibration

Whether deploying a new office multifunction printer, replacing toner cartridges, or troubleshooting streaked lines on an inkjet printer, running a dedicated calibration test page is the standard diagnostic procedure. Our test page renders pure CSS/SVG vector elements designed to expose mechanical and chemical printing flaws.

❓ How do I identify and fix inkjet nozzle clogs?

Inspect the four primary color bars (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Black). If you see horizontal white streaks or missing patches across a specific color, that color's printhead nozzles are partially blocked by dried ink. Run your printer's built-in "Head Cleaning" utility and reprint the test sheet.

❓ Why must I check "Background graphics" in the print dialog?

By default, web browsers disable background colors and CSS gradients to save ink when printing normal web articles. To print our diagnostic color blocks and grayscale wedges, you must enable "Background graphics" (or "Print backgrounds") in your browser's print options.

❓ How do laser printers use the grayscale step wedge?

Laser printers use electrostatic drums and toner fusing rollers. The 10-step grayscale bar tests your toner drum's ability to maintain clear contrast differentiation between 10% dark gray and 90% near-black without muddiness or ghosting shadows.
