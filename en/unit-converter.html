📖 Unit Systems & Conversion Standards Guide

Physical measurement units are standardized by the International System of Units (SI) based on seven base units (meter, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, and candela). For engineering, trade, aviation, and culinary needs across the US, UK, and international markets, converting between Metric (SI), Imperial, and US Customary systems is an essential daily workflow.

❓ What is the difference between Metric Ton, Long Ton, and Short Ton?

A Metric Ton (t) equals exactly 1,000 kilograms (~2,204.62 lbs). The US Short Ton equals 2,000 pounds (~907.18 kg), while the Imperial Long Ton equals 2,240 pounds (~1,016.05 kg).

❓ Why are US Fluid Ounces and British Imperial Ounces different?

In the US Customary system, 1 US Gallon equals 128 US fluid ounces (~3.785 liters), meaning 1 US fl oz is ~29.57 ml. In the British Imperial system, 1 Imperial Gallon equals 160 UK fluid ounces (~4.546 liters), making 1 UK fl oz ~28.41 ml.

❓ How does the temperature conversion formula work?

Unlike length or mass, temperature scales do not share a zero-point origin.
• Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
• Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9
• Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15

❓ Binary vs Decimal storage prefixes: KB vs KiB?

Under the decimal standard (SI), 1 KB = 1,000 Bytes (commonly used by hard drive manufacturers). Under the binary computing standard (IEC), 1 KiB (or traditional computer OS KB) = 1,024 Bytes. Our tool uses binary 1,024 byte multipliers matching operating system memory views.
