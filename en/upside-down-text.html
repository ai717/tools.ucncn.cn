📖 What is Upside Down Text? Unicode Glyphs & Inversion Guide

Upside down text (sometimes called flipped text) uses specialized Unicode characters that naturally resemble inverted versions of Latin alphabet letters and numbers. For instance, the inverted equivalent of a is the International Phonetic Alphabet turn ɐ, while e maps to ǝ.

❓ Why does upside down text flip both vertically and horizontally?

In traditional 180° rotation, text is rotated around its central axis. If you rotate a sheet of paper upside down, the first letter ends up at the bottom right. Our default 180° mode automatically reverses the letter sequence so that reading from left-to-right matches a physical paper turn. We also provide an "In-Place Inverted" mode if you wish to keep original character positions.

❓ Can I post upside down text on Twitter, TikTok, and Discord?

Yes! Because these inverted characters are standard Unicode symbols, they can be copied and pasted directly into social media tweets, Discord messages, YouTube comments, and game tags.

❓ Are numbers and punctuation marks inverted as well?

Yes. Common punctuation marks and numerals have inverted Unicode counterparts, such as ¿ (inverted question mark), ¡ (inverted exclamation mark), and ˙ (raised dot).
