📖 Text Analytics Guide: Words vs Characters & Presentation Pacing

Whether authoring academic papers, drafting high-impact keynote presentations, or writing SEO meta descriptions, staying within character and word limits is essential. Different platforms evaluate text differently—some measure raw characters including whitespace, while others count distinct words.

❓ What is the standard silent reading speed vs speaking speed?


• Silent Reading: The average adult reads silently at approximately 200–250 words per minute (wpm).
• Public Speaking / Presentations: Professional keynote speakers and announcers pace their delivery at 120–140 wpm to ensure audience comprehension and natural breathing pauses.

❓ What are common social media character limits?


• Twitter / X: 280 characters for standard accounts.
• Google SEO Meta Description: 155–160 characters (truncates beyond ~960 pixels).
• Google Search Title Tag: 55–60 characters (truncates beyond ~600 pixels).
• Instagram Caption: 2,200 characters (displays first 125 characters before "more").

❓ How does the word counter handle hyphenated words and contractions?

Standard words are segmented by whitespace and punctuation boundaries. Common contractions (e.g., "don't", "it's") and hyphenated terms (e.g., "state-of-the-art") are recognized as single linguistic word tokens.
